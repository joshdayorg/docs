## Build Your Own AI Operations Center

This tutorial walks you through setting up OpenClaw from scratch and building it into something genuinely useful: a personal AI that knows you, runs 24/7, and can actually do things on your behalf.

By the end, you'll have multiple AI agents running on your own machine, connected to your chat apps, managing your inbox, monitoring your work tools, and acting autonomously on your schedule.

**This isn't theory.** Every technique covered here comes from a real production setup running 7+ agents across iMessage, Slack, Discord, and the web.
