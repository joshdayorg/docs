## Skip the hard part.

This tutorial teaches you OpenClaw by building with it. No theory dumps. No 47-page architecture overviews. You install it, you configure it, you watch it work.

By the end, you'll have multiple AI agents running on your own machine, connected to your chat apps, handling real tasks on your schedule. Every technique here comes from a production setup running 7+ agents across iMessage, Slack, Discord, and the web.

**You just need a Mac, 20 minutes, and an API key.**
